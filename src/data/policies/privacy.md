## 1. Regulatory Status and Scope

Orchiddigital Consulting Limited ("Orchid", "the Company", "we", "our", or "us") is duly registered and regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Nigeria, as a Fund and Portfolio Manager.

This Privacy Policy is issued in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) (where applicable), and relevant SEC rules governing client data confidentiality and investor protection.

This Policy applies to all clients, prospective clients, website users, and other stakeholders whose personal data is processed by Orchid.

## 2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

Orchid may collect, process, and store the following categories of data:

- **Personal Identification Data:** Full name, date of birth, gender, nationality
- **Contact Information:** Residential address, email address, telephone numbers
- **Financial Data:** Bank account details, BVN, transaction history, investment records
- **Regulatory/KYC Data:** Government-issued identification, utility bills, tax information
- **Technical Data:** IP address, login data, browser type, device identifiers
- **Communication Records:** Correspondence, instructions, complaints, and feedback

## 3. Purpose and Lawful Basis of Processing

Personal data shall be processed strictly for legitimate and lawful purposes, including:

- Execution of contractual obligations for investment and portfolio management services
- Compliance with statutory and regulatory obligations (including SEC, AML/CFT laws)
- Client onboarding, due diligence, and identity verification
- Processing of transactions, redemptions, and payouts
- Risk management, fraud detection, and internal controls
- Provision of client reporting and performance updates

Processing shall be based on:

- Contractual necessity
- Legal obligation
- Legitimate business interest
- Consent (where required under NDPR/NDPA)

## 4. Data Disclosure and Third-Party Processing

Orchid may disclose personal data strictly on a need-to-know basis to:

- SEC-approved custodians and financial institutions
- Regulatory authorities (SEC, EFCC, NFIU, CBN, etc.)
- External auditors, legal advisers, and compliance consultants
- Technology vendors and data processors under binding confidentiality agreements

All third parties are required to comply with applicable data protection laws and maintain adequate safeguards.

## 5. Data Security and Integrity

Orchid shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data, including:

- Encryption protocols and secure data storage systems
- Access controls and role-based authorization
- Continuous monitoring, audit trails, and system security reviews

## 6. Data Retention Policy

Personal data shall be retained in accordance with:

- SEC record-keeping requirements
- Applicable financial regulations
- Operational and legal obligations

Data shall not be retained longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

## 7. Data Subject Rights

In accordance with NDPR/NDPA, data subjects shall have the right to:

- Request access to personal data held
- Request correction or rectification of inaccurate data
- Request deletion or restriction (subject to regulatory obligations)
- Withdraw consent where applicable

Requests shall be submitted through Orchid's designated communication channels.

## 8. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria, Orchid shall ensure such transfers comply with NDPR requirements and that adequate data protection safeguards are in place.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Orchid website may utilize cookies and similar technologies for operational, analytical, and security purposes. Users may manage cookie preferences through browser settings.

## 10. Complaints and Data Protection Enquiries

All data protection complaints or enquiries shall be directed to Orchid via its official communication channels. Complaints shall be handled in accordance with applicable regulations and internal escalation procedures.

## 11. Amendments

Orchid reserves the right to amend this Policy at any time. Updated versions shall be published on the website.
